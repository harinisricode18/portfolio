01 — About

Engineering at the intersection of the body and the algorithm.

I'm a biomedical engineering student building intelligent systems that combine embedded sensing, machine learning, and AI-driven decision pipelines — with projects spanning biomedical signal analysis, wearable diagnostics, and healthcare AI applications.

My work sits deliberately close to the patient: an ESP32 knee brace that reads joint stability in real time, a respiratory profiler that fuses acoustic and physiological signals, and clinical shadowing across seven hospital departments that taught me how devices actually behave in the hands of clinicians. I care about applied AI/ML, multimodal data fusion, and building agentic systems for real-world clinical decision-making.
